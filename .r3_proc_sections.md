## Paste-ready executive procurement verdict

The independent procurement/data-semantics pass found no fabricated human
labels and no usable tender-level external validation. It did, however, find
seven P1 defects that made the pre-remediation generation unsuitable for
acceptance: 291 design contests were classified as ordinary open/restricted
procedures; published source-party identities were discarded in supplier and
HHI calculations; award deduplication collapsed one demonstrably distinct
award; 6,107 absent bidder arrays were asserted to mean zero bidders; manual
audit URL coverage was overstated and force regeneration could erase future
human work; and user-facing text repeatedly described award decisions and
award values as contracts and contracted expenditure. Seven further P2 defects
affected identifier hygiene, lot semantics, legal/value diagnostics,
external-evidence provenance, and audit metadata.

The actionable source defects have now been remediated and targeted no-write
recomputation is internally consistent. The principal quantitative changes
are 62,128 → 62,129 canonical award rows, +EUR 7,200 recorded award value,
9,427 → 9,940 supplier-summary rows, 291 explicit design contests, and the
replacement of the false 6,107-row `ZERO_BIDDER` population by an honest
6,107-row missing-publication population. These are material corrections; the
old figures must not be preserved merely because they appear in the thesis.

Procurement acceptance nevertheless remains **pending**, not granted, until
the canonical release chain and all reports, notebooks, dashboard readers,
manual-audit metadata, benchmark, and ML artifacts have been regenerated and
validated as one generation. Several questions cannot responsibly be closed
by code: there are no completed human review labels, no linkable OSHP tender
outcomes, no stable award-to-lot identifier, no externally verified supplier
or buyer entity resolution, and insufficient source evidence to decide the
legality of the seven price-quotation/value conflicts or any tender deadline.
The project may support descriptive statistics, transparent rule-based
screening, and snapshot-relative anomaly ranking; it does not support fraud,
corruption, legal-breach, causal, precision/recall, false-positive-rate, or
temporal-generalisation claims.

## Primary legal/procedure source ledger

1. [PPRC, consolidated Regulation No. 001/2022 as amended by Regulation No. 002/2024 (official Albanian PDF)](https://e-prokurimi.rks-gov.net/HOME/Documents/Legislation/Rregulloret/shq/versioni%20i%20konsoliduar%20i%20Rregullores%20Nr.%20001%202022%20i%20plot%C3%ABsuar%20dhe%20ndryshuar%20me%20Rregulloren%20Nr.%20002%202024.pdf)
   — Article 12.3 maps electronic procedure codes 1–8 in the current
   consolidated numbering; Article 51.3 governs price-quotation applicability;
   Article 51.5 supplies its five-day minimum period. The original 2022
   regulation used Article 12.13, so current documentation should cite 12.3
   and may explain the renumbering when discussing older review text.

2. [PPRC, 2016 Rules and Operational Guidelines for Public Procurement (official English predecessor)](https://e-prokurimi.rks-gov.net/HOME/Documents/Legislation/Aktet%20dyt%C3%ABsore%20te%20shfuqizuara/eng/A01%20Rules%20and%20Operational%20Guidelines%20for%20Public%20Procurement.pdf)
   — the annex maps codes 1–7 for the historical period. It is used only to
   interpret historical source semantics, not as current law and not to apply
   2024 text retroactively.

3. [Kosovo Public Procurement Law, consolidated official English text](https://e-prokurimi.rks-gov.net/Home/Documents/Legislation/Ligjet/eng/PPL%20consolidated.pdf)
   — Article 19 defines low value as at least EUR1,000 and below EUR10,000 and
   minimum value as below EUR1,000; Article 73 separately governs design
   contests. Articles 44–46 show why a generic short-deadline screen is not a
   legal-breach test: time limits depend on procedure/value and may be affected
   by accelerated procedure or prior-information conditions that this snapshot
   does not fully encode.

4. [PPRC, original English Regulation No. 001/2022](https://e-prokurimi.rks-gov.net/HOME/Documents/Legislation/SecondaryLegislation/eng/A01%20Regulation%20No.001_2022%20on%20Public%20Procurement.pdf)
   — useful for the original English wording and former Article 12.13
   numbering, but the consolidated amended text above is the current reference.

## Remaining human/external-evidence limits

- Human review is incomplete: no reviewer labels exist, so no precision,
  recall, false-positive rate, inter-rater agreement or outcome calibration may
  be reported. Blank fields mean unreviewed, never negative.
- The `rule_only` audit stratum was empty in the pre-rebuild population. It must
  remain empty if the final population is empty; no synthetic examples may be
  inserted.
- The current 150-row pre-rebuild audit sample contains 31 source releases
  without a usable exact document URL. No alternate link may be invented.
- OSHP evidence is not linkable to individual tenders and contains no adequate
  adjudicated outcome label. Aggregate authority/operator name overlap is not
  validation.
- The seven quotation/value conflicts require the original notice, amendments,
  applicable historical rules and surrounding lot/framework facts. They are
  not findings of illegality.
- Buyer-ID/name collision truth requires authoritative entity-history evidence.
  Automatic name merging would itself risk corruption.
- Source supplier party IDs are publication identifiers, not independently
  verified business/legal identifiers. Name fallback may merge homonyms and
  party IDs may split one legal entity; HHI is therefore sensitivity analysis,
  not a relevant-market measure.
- Awards and tender lots lack a shared stable lot identifier. Exact-title
  statuses are QA evidence only; tender-level ratios cannot establish lot-level
  allocation.
- Deadline legality cannot be inferred without period-specific law, urgency,
  prior notice, invitation/publication, corrigenda and amendment evidence.
- OCDS award objects do not prove signed, executed or paid contracts.
- A static, unlabeled snapshot cannot establish causation, fraud/corruption,
  temporal generalisation, online score stability or national-census coverage.
