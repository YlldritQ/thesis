# R3 clean-room procurement/data-semantics audit

## Independence declaration and scope

This is the Phase 1 independent procurement-semantics audit. I did **not** open, read, grep, stat, or otherwise consult `THESIS_REVIEW_R2.md` or `THESIS_REVIEW_R2.1.md`. I made no repository or artifact changes other than creating this note. Findings below were derived from current source code, current Parquet/JSON/CSV artifacts, representative raw records, and primary official legal sources.

Scope: procedure taxonomy and applicability; deadline/value-threshold semantics; tender/award/lot/supplier/buyer identifiers; missingness, deduplication, joins, and aggregation grain; supplier fallback and HHI; manual audit/source URLs; OSHP provenance and linkability; and the distinction between award decisions and contracts.

## Examiner verdict

The procurement layer is not presently acceptance-ready. The core tender and award joins are internally strong and the project correctly avoids calling its screens fraud/corruption validation, but six material defects affect the meaning of published results: design contests are classified as standard/restricted procurement procedures; supplier party identifiers are discarded even when they are the only published identity; award deduplication collapses one demonstrably distinct award; absent tenderer data is represented as an observed zero; manual-audit URL coverage is materially overstated and force regeneration can erase later human labels; and the dashboard/narrative repeatedly relabel award decisions and award values as signed contracts/contracted value. These are genuine P1 defects, not merely thesis caveats.

No evidence supports a finding of fabricated labels or fabricated external validation. Manual labels are blank, the empty `rule_only` stratum is honestly retained, and OSHP tender-level validation is explicitly withdrawn. Legal compliance cannot be inferred from this static release: urgency, prior information notices, corrigenda, invitations, framework participants, and contract execution documents are not systematically available.

## Legal/procedure applicability checks

Primary official sources consulted:

1. PPRC, **Regulation No. 001/2022 on Public Procurement**: <https://e-prokurimi.rks-gov.net/HOME/Documents/Legislation/SecondaryLegislation/eng/A01%20Regulation%20No.001_2022%20on%20Public%20Procurement.pdf>
   - Article 12.13 (PDF pp. 16-17): official electronic procedure codes 1-8, including code 3 “Design contest.”
   - Article 51.5 (PDF p. 99): price-quotation tender deadline is at least five days from publication.
   - Article 52 (PDF p. 100): minimum-value procedure below EUR 1,000; one responsive bid may suffice after the required invitations.
   - Article 54.23-54.42 (PDF pp. 106-109): mini-competition is among framework-agreement operators and the current minimum tender period is 10 days.
   - Article 55 (PDF p. 109): design-contest definition and application.
2. PPRC, **Law on Public Procurement, consolidated text**: <https://e-prokurimi.rks-gov.net/Home/Documents/Legislation/Ligjet/eng/PPL%20consolidated.pdf>
   - Article 19 (PDF p. 19): low-value band is EUR 1,000 to below EUR 10,000; minimum value is below EUR 1,000.
   - Articles 44-46 (PDF pp. 35-37): ordinary and accelerated time limits and prior-information-notice reductions.
   - Article 73 (PDF pp. 54-55): design contests are a separate Title III procedure.

The zero-weight `ULTRA_SHORT_OPEN` screen is appropriately described as a non-statutory diagnostic in `src/thesis/anomaly/rules.py:138-151,235-236`. A generic seven-day cutoff must not be called a legal breach. Ordinary open deadlines vary by value and can be shortened for urgency or prior information; the snapshot lacks reliable urgency, prior-notice, corrigendum, and invitation evidence. Price quotation is correctly excluded from that rule. Mini-competition is also correctly outside it. The immediate defect is the two design contests admitted by PR-01, not the cautious zero weighting itself.

- Current table shapes: Silver tenders 75,710 x 26; Silver awards 62,128 x 25; Gold tender features 75,710 x 75; Gold tender summary 75,710 x 88; supplier summary 9,427 x 19; procuring-entity summary 206 x 20.

- Award integrity: all canonical award IDs/uIDs are nonnull and unique; values are nonnull/nonnegative; all currencies are EUR; 8,760 award dates are missing (14.10%). There are 54,133 tenders with recorded awards and 21,577 without a recorded award.
- Lots: one tender has no published lot array; 69,579 have one lot; 6,130 have multiple lots.
- `num_awards` currently agrees with the current deduplicated award-row count per tender. No raw award object contains multiple suppliers in this snapshot, so award-to-supplier explosion does **not** currently duplicate monetary value. That suspected failure mode was tested and rejected.
- Winner concentration is correctly framed in current methodology as descriptive rather than a relevant-market competition measure. The defect is supplier identity/denominator sensitivity (PR-02), not a claim that national HHI proves market dominance.
- The external OSHP artifact contains no tender-level outcome metrics, and documentation says validation is unavailable. No aggregate overlap is misrepresented as tender-level validation.
- The manual sample preserves the genuinely empty `rule_only` stratum and has no inferred or generated reviewer labels.

## Required separation of defects from unavailable evidence

Actionable implementation defects are PR-01 through PR-07 and PR-10/PR-13. PR-08, PR-09, PR-11, and PR-12 require the indicated safeguards and honest sensitivity/provenance work, but their underlying legal/entity/linkage truth cannot responsibly be invented:

- The seven price-quotation/value conflicts require original procedure and amendment documents; they are not proof of illegality.
- Buyer-ID collisions require authoritative entity-history evidence before merging.
- Unmatched/duplicate lot titles require stable source lot IDs or document review.
- Current OSHP data cannot validate tender outcomes; richer official documents must be acquired before linkage.
- Deadline legality requires procedure-specific publication/invitation, urgency, prior-notice, corrigendum, and amendment evidence absent from the snapshot.
- `supplier_party_id` is strong published source identity for within-snapshot sensitivity, but it is not independently verified as a legal business identifier.

## Clean-room acceptance blockers

Procurement/data-semantics acceptance should remain blocked until the P1 items are fixed and every dependent Silver/Gold/report/model/dashboard artifact is regenerated: explicit design-contest taxonomy; supplier-ID and HHI sensitivity; supplier-aware award dedup; nullable bidder-observation semantics; structurally valid manual-review URLs and label-safe regeneration; and award-not-contract language. Legal compliance, human review outcomes, buyer legal-entity merges, and OSHP tender outcomes must remain explicitly unresolved unless primary evidence is later acquired.
