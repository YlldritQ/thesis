4. **R2-21 combines leakage magnitude, documentation, and a new serving architecture.** The small current leakage sensitivity is quantified and all live/temporal claims are withdrawn, but frozen versioned statistics do not exist; partial is the only accurate single disposition.

It is “Already fixed and verified,” while possible over-weighting from correlated raw/missingness features remains a distinct R3 sensitivity issue.
