**R2-1 · Blank supplier IDs corrupt every shipped concentration statistic — the "top-1 works supplier with 63.7%" is the empty string** — NEW (defect inside the H-6 fix) · `src/thesis/reporting.py:79-88` · reporters: domain, dataeng · effort **M**
18,274 of 62,128 award rows (29.4%) — 13,723 with `supplier_business_id == ''` plus 4,551 NaN — carry **51.75% of all awarded value** (€2,466.4m). `reporting.py` groups by the raw ID: pandas drops the NaN keys and pools the `''` rows into one phantom "supplier", which is the **top-1 supplier by value in all three categories** — works 63.7% (€1,718.2m, 2,801 distinct firms), services 37.4% (1,358 firms), goods 21.6% (1,209 firms). 0.637² × 10⁴ ≈ 4,058 ≈ the shipped works HHI 4,067: the "highly concentrated works market" headline in findings §3 *is* the blob. **Q3. "Give me the confidence interval on the 1.48."**
Honest answer: with supplier clustering, the 95% CI on the shipped lift definition is ≈ [0.98, 2.20] — it includes 1.0, and removing one supplier moves the point to 1.33. *Suggestive, not statistically significant.* The defensible external result is the negative one: the rule-flag lift is cluster-robust (0.64, CI [0.51, 0.81]).

**Q4. "Two of your rules cannot fire on your disqualified set by construction — is 0.65 comparable at all?"**
Honest answer: no. Awarded-only baseline gives 0.74; single-bidder-stratified ≈ 0.90. The per-rule decomposition shows the ratio/timing rules track OSHP at ~1.4–2× while single-bidding anti-tracks compositionally. **Q5. "Which labels elected your ensemble — the ones you shipped, or the ones you deleted?"**
Honest answer today: the pre-reweight labels, which no longer ship; the fingerprints prove it and re-running moves proxy-AUC by +0.116. **Q6. "You produced 22,480 ML-only flags and demoted 5,219 of your own rules' flags — did you ever hand-check even fifty?"**
